# Research Question Change Report

## Summary of Change

**Old Research Question (R2):**
> "What is the potential of modeling dialogue in interaction design to enable effective human–AI co-creativity?"

**New Research Question (R2):**
> "How to enable co-creative dialogues between humans and AI from the perspective of interaction design?"

## Conceptual Shift

The reformulation represents a shift from:
- **Exploratory framing** ("What is the potential...") → **Prescriptive/action-oriented framing** ("How to enable...")
- **Investigating possibility** → **Providing guidance on implementation**
- **Assessing value** → **Designing solutions**
