\chapter{Integrating Generative AI into Creative Workflows: Dealing with Consistency, Scene Control and Refinement in a Professional Image Generation Case Study} \label{c:tc6} 

\section{Link to Thesis}
In this chapter, I continue traversing the interaction design space of dialogic human-AI co-creativity, now turning my attention to the field of image generation. This chapter presents a case study in real-world creative production: a collaboration with the Australian Financial Review to produce visuals for their annual Power Issue.
Through this case study, I seek to analyse the dialogic co-creative affordances of an image generation system, how these influence roles, and the effectiveness of the interaction. Addressing RQ2, I found that this system was very limited in terms of dialogic interaction, offering only prompt-generation functionality with minimal support for iteration or refinement. However, by allowing us to train the model on custom datasets, the system enabled a form of meta-dialogue: a way to iteratively guide the model towards our creative objectives. This process contributed to mutual influence and understanding, albeit at a level above the immediate interaction.
